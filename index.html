01 — Breeding philosophy

Flavor first.

Weight comes from how you grow. I don't breed for it.

I chase the plant that stops me in the row.

Rub a stem. Smell your fingers. That's the selection.

Most don't make it. The few that do get years.

02 — The spiritual side

What goes in, comes out.

I'm out there at dawn, hands in the soil.

Quiet. Paying attention.

No magic. Just presence, repeated.

I think the plants know the difference. I know I do.
